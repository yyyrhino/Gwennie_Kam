作品主體為藝術家臨摹早年由英國皇家音樂學院出版的《舒伯特：三首鋼琴曲 D.946》封面，是其青少年時期學習西方音樂的曲譜之一。透過以繪畫複製印刷品，藝術家意在反思在人生的學習過程中，我們以模仿的手段，一次又一次地試圖接近前人的思想和經驗。我們奢望著完美的複製粘貼，以獲得「原真」的話語。然而，在時代的加速滑輪中，我們將模仿能力拱手於人工智能的同時，卻又顧忌著人的「原真」能力和知識被僭越而感到彷徨。當原真性不復存在，不厭其煩地強調並詮釋它的人們，又是在嚮往著何種權力？我們憂慮被人工智能所取代，在反身來模仿靠攏它的時候，是否又反映著我們深究文化的精神正在慢慢消逝？

Just as if she were performing the piece while painting, the artist creates a visual interpretation of "Schubert: Three Piano Pieces, D. 946" in this artwork. She references an earlier version published by the Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music. Having received professional piano training since childhood, the artist engages in repetitive and immersive imitation, a process widely regarded as crucial for capturing composers' "true" style in Western music. Through the performance of replicating prints with paint, the artist contemplates the pursuit of a "perfectly authentic" copy in countless learning experiences throughout our lives. We often strive to closely approximate predecessors’ thoughts, wishing to gain power and authority. Driven by accelerationism, we quickly cede our imitative capabilities to artificial intelligence, but we soon find ourselves in the throes of an authenticity crisis. What is the purpose of clinging to the claim of authenticity? Does it not signify a loss of our spirit in the profound exploration of culture if we imitate AI in an attempt to calm our fear of being replaced?
