藝術家、策展人、音樂人類學學者。成長於香港，畢業於香港中文大學音樂系，主修鋼琴演奏，後於美國夏威夷大學研究院修習音樂人類學。在她的當代藝術實踐中，以「噪音」作為自我角色的詮釋與創作命題，不斷嘗試突破生澀的學術話語與文化原生社會間的隔閡，並從時空和材質等維度揭示聲音中的公共性，探索聲音是如何被運用於想像、創造、定義和表演文化認同。
金佐寧參展的項目包括：「地方音景：蘇州的聲音地理」（蘇州，2021）；「原音：太原的地方聲景」（太原，2021）；2022「燃冉」藝術季 —— 迴環之歌（上海，2022）等。作為策展人，她曾入圍 2021 OCAT 研究中心「研究型展覽計劃」及 2023 集美・阿爾勒「影像策展人獎」終選名單。2022 年，金佐寧成為德英基金會首屆策展學者，出版論文「錯過的約會 —— 重返聲音的擴展場域」。


2023 至 24 年度，金佐寧獲選為「燃冉」駐留藝術家，同時受香港大館當代美術館邀請作「布魯斯．瑙曼」展覽講座「另類視聽：從丑角到噪音」之主講嘉賓。其文章及展評寫作常見於《信睿週報》、Brooklyn Rail、ARTnews（中文版）及《藝術當代》等。

Gwendoline Cho-ning Kam (b. Hong Kong; currently works and lives in Shanghai and Hong Kong) is an artist, curator, and ethnomusicologist. She holds a Bachelor of Arts in Music from The Chinese University of Hong Kong and pursued further studies in ethnomusicology at the University of Hawaii at Mānoa. Kam’s practice explores how multi-sensory interactions in auditory narratives shape everyday experiences, perceptions, and expressions of materiality. In her contemporary artworks, she employs noise as both a self-referential concept and creative theme, bridging academic discourse with cultural communities. Through investigations of time, space, and materials, she interrogates the public dimensions of sound and examines how this medium is manipulated to imagine, shape, define, and perform cultural identities.

Kam has participated in exhibitions including Native Soundscape: The Sonic Geography of Suzhou (Suzhou, 2021), Acoustic Taiyuan: A Native Soundscape Project (Taiyuan, 2021), and 2022 RanRan Songs of the Return Art Season (Shanghai, 2022). As a curator, she was shortlisted for the OCAT Institute’s Research-Based Curatorial Project (2021) and the JIMEI x ARLES Curatorial Award for Photography and Moving Image (2023). In 2022, she became an inaugural curatorial fellow of the De Ying Foundation and published her research paper "The Missed Rendezvous: Returning to the Expanded Field of Sound." In 2023, she was selected for the XINTIANDI × UCCA RanRan Artists-in-Residence Program. Her writings and exhibition critiques have appeared in The Thinker, Brooklyn Rail, ARTnews (China), ArtChina, etc.
