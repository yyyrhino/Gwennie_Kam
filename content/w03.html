‘F’ to Bill


裝置作品「F」to Bill 以傳統崑曲主奏樂器板鼓及與其協作的銅響器作為裝置的主體，結合現代形制、被“噤聲”的“透明揚聲器”，傳達崑曲鑼鼓在以視覺為主導的當代社會關係中的處境。樂器後方為一系列與昆曲鑼鼓文化相關的當代藝術概念，其與崑曲的“固化”標籤並置，將觀眾置身於對話場域；作品中的文本設計參照手抄崑曲工尺譜的形式，以中英對照呈現，反諷在當代消費主義之下，人們盲目與西方 “跨界” 對傳統文化所造成的扭曲與威脅，亦造成本已存在的聽覺文化走向消亡。

顯示屏幕，鑼鼓（板鼓，大鑼，鐃鈸，小鑼），亞克力板，

The installation 'F' to Bill centers on the ban gu—a primary instrument in traditional Kunqu opera—and its accompanying bronze percussion tools. Paired with these are modernly designed, "silenced" transparent speakers, together articulating the plight of Kunqu's percussion traditions within a contemporary society dominated by visual culture.


Behind the instruments lies a series of contemporary art concepts tied to Kunqu's percussion heritage. Juxtaposed with the "stereotypical labels" imposed on Kunqu, these concepts place viewers in a space of dialogue. The text within the work, modeled after the form of hand-copied Kunqu gongche notation and presented in parallel Chinese and English, ironizes how, under modern consumerism, blind pursuits of "cross-cultural" fusion with the West distort and threaten traditional culture, while also hastening the decline of an already fragile auditory heritage.
